## Categories

> Give at least 5 categories. At least one must be numbered (Type: number or Type: time).
> Every plain category needs exactly 8 items, separated by commas. No item may appear twice
> anywhere in the topic, and no item may contain a cast name.
> Sentence and Not sentence must contain {x}, which the game replaces with the item.
> Leads: at least 2 per category. Each lead must contain {x}. You may also use {one} (the cast
> word) and {prize} (what is at stake).
> Numbered categories:
>   Type: number   then Start, Step (8 values), Unit (one, many), Fewer phrase, More phrase
>   Type: time     then Start time (like 9:00 AM), Step minutes, Earlier phrase, Later phrase
> Phrases are what a clue says between two names: "arrived earlier than", "walked more miles than".
> Avoid higher/lower and above/below; they need an extra note saying which way numbers run.

## Story parts

> Each line is one part. Write at least the number asked for; more is better.
> Openings must use {n} or {N} (the head count) and {many} (the cast word, plural).
> Never name a category item or a cast name in any part: it would read like a clue.
> Endings must use {Fact} (the answer) and may use {winner} and {prize}.
> Keep each part to about 10 to 25 words.
