Get the Book Studio app

The app opens Book Studio in its own window, works without a connection once you have signed in (tick "Keep me signed in"), and syncs your books with your account. Sign in with the same email everywhere and your books follow you.

Windows Download for Windows Open the file to install it; it updates itself. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed with a paid certificate: click More info, then Run anyway.
This device In Chrome or Edge (Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Android), open the browser menu and choose Install Book Studio (or Apps, then Install). On iPhone or iPad, in Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On a Mac in Safari: choose File, then Add to Dock.
