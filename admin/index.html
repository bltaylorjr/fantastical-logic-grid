Put the dashboard on your phone or computer

It installs like an app, free, with no app store.

  • Windows: download the Windows app and open the file to install it. It updates itself. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app is not signed with a paid certificate: click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Windows or Mac in Chrome or Edge: click Install the app at the top of this page, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  • iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

With Keep me signed in on this device ticked, it stays signed in while you use other apps. After 1 hour without use it locks, and Face ID or your fingerprint opens it again (set that up under Face ID and fingerprint once you are in).

Use the break-glass key

For emergencies only, for example if sign-in is down. This is the Worker's ADMIN_TOKEN secret. It has the owner's rights and every use is logged.

Daily puzzles

Everyone gets the same daily puzzle. Change a coming day's theme, difficulty or size, or ask for a new story. Past days and today never change, and no story is ever used twice. The site is rebuilt every night just after midnight US Eastern, so a change shows in the preview and the game after the next build.

Characters

The game's own characters. A published character joins new puzzles as one of the cast or tells the story, with the face, voice and lines you give them, in every theme or only the ones you choose. Names and lines are checked against the story rules and the word filter before publishing, so puzzles stay fair and stories never repeat. Portraits and line drafts use Cloudflare Workers AI's free daily allowance; drawn faces are free and work offline.

                Error tracking

                Every Report a Problem, sorted by category as it arrives: Puzzle (PZ-…) or Web structure (WS-…), with any pictures and files the player attached. The puzzle doctor reviews each report the moment it is submitted: it checks a reported puzzle within a minute or two (or at the latest in its 15-minute round), rebuilds a broken one, emails the player who asked, and sends you an alert with the outcome. Every step is kept in the errors database below. It uses Web structure reports from the same screen and app version to tell a broken puzzle from a display problem, and learns which repairs work for each kind of fault.

                Loading…

                What the doctor has learned

                One row per kind of fault. For puzzles: the fault, theme and difficulty, how often it was seen and repaired, the rebuild that works best, and the doctor's latest self-check with the newest engine. For web pages: the screen and app version. When a self-check shows a fault is gone, its waiting reports are repaired and emailed automatically.

                Errors database: every step

                Each report's steps, newest first: received, files attached, the doctor started, its review, the repair or why it could not repair, the alert to you, and how it was closed. Nothing personal is kept here. Steps are kept for a year; attached files for 90 days.

                Puzzle submissions

                Every puzzle players sent in, old and new: Puzzle Wizard puzzles and storylines, in four sections. Awaiting approval is new and resubmitted entries, Awaiting edits is entries the team asked the author to change, then Approved and Published. Open an entry to edit it, email the player who sent it (from support@thelogicgrid.com), push its puzzle to their Saved puzzles in the Logic Grid app, move it to another section, or delete it. Full review opens it on the Storyline submissions page for the trademark scan and corrected wording.

                  Storyline submissions

                  The Storyline Submission Database: every theme idea, category idea and storyline players sent, from the game's Suggest a storyline page and from Share an idea, kept exactly as they sent it, with the player, their author credit, their consent and the first puzzle made from an approved storyline. Open an entry to see the original form. Scan checks it for trademarks, protected characters and copied wording (a screening aid, not legal advice). Approve and build puzzle builds the first puzzle from the corrected words, saves it here and in the player's Saved puzzles, and emails it to the author as a PDF. Confirmations, approvals and decline thank-yous go from noreply@thelogicgrid.com; copies you email go from support@thelogicgrid.com.
