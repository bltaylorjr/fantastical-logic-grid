Put the dashboard on your phone or computer

It installs like an app, free, with no app store.

  • Windows: download the Windows app and open the file to install it. It updates itself. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app is not signed with a paid certificate: click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Windows or Mac in Chrome or Edge: click Install the app at the top of this page, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  • iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

You sign in each time you open it, as on the web.

Use the break-glass key

For emergencies only, for example if sign-in is down. This is the Worker's ADMIN_TOKEN secret. It has the owner's rights and every use is logged.

Daily puzzles

Everyone gets the same daily puzzle. Change a coming day's theme, difficulty or size, or ask for a new story. Past days and today never change, and no story is ever used twice. The site is rebuilt every night at 00:17 UTC, so a change shows in the preview and the game after the next build.

To publish a change sooner, run Publish web version on GitHub (Run workflow).

Characters

The game's own characters. A published character joins new puzzles as one of the cast or tells the story, with the face, voice and lines you give them, in every theme or only the ones you choose. Names and lines are checked against the story rules and the word filter before publishing, so puzzles stay fair and stories never repeat. Portraits and line drafts use Cloudflare Workers AI's free daily allowance; drawn faces are free and work offline.

      Puzzle emails

      Players and guests sign up in the game (Menu > Puzzle emails) for the puzzle of the week (Mondays from about 7 am Eastern, a bigger Expert puzzle) and special edition puzzles you plan below. Guests confirm with a link first. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Sent every 15 minutes by the Puzzle emails GitHub Action, from noreply@thelogicgrid.com through Resend, or from the game's Gmail as a backup.

      Story workshop and review

      Story workshop

      Write story parts that every player's new stories use. The game mixes them with its own, so a story is never repeated. Story parts only change the words, never the logic, and every part is checked by the game's rules first. Added parts reach players within about 15 minutes.

      AI story writer

      Describe an idea and Google Gemini drafts a few parts to choose from (free tier, using the GEMINI_API_KEY Cloudflare secret). Nothing is added until you press Add.

        Shared by players

        Story parts sent from older versions of the game, and the parts added here. Approved parts reach every player within about 15 minutes. Rejected parts are never shown. Parts are anonymous.

          Communication

          Everything players send and the game reports, in one inbox, and the messages the team sends back. Players see the team's messages in the game under Menu > Messages, signed as "The team".

          Part

            Ideas and problems are anonymous unless the player ticked "Let the team reply to me"; then they show a Reply button. Error reports are anonymous and have no done state: New shows the last 7 days, All the last 30. Done items are deleted after 180 days.

            Message one player

            It appears in their Messages in the game. They can reply, and the conversation shows in the inbox.

            News in every player's Messages

            A longer note than a banner, for everyone who opens Messages, signed in or not. The newest five show.

              Banner on the home screen

              A short message at the top of the game's home screen. Players can close it. Up to three show at once.

                Error tracking

                Every Report a Problem, sorted by category as it arrives: Puzzle (PZ-…) or Web structure (WS-…). The puzzle doctor checks Puzzle reports every 15 minutes, rebuilds the broken ones and emails the players who asked. It uses Web structure reports from the same screen and app version to tell a broken puzzle from a display problem, and learns which repairs work for each kind of fault.

                What the doctor has learned

                One row per kind of fault. For puzzles: the fault, theme and difficulty, how often it was seen and repaired, the rebuild that works best, and the doctor's latest self-check with the newest engine. For web pages: the screen and app version. When a self-check shows a fault is gone, its waiting reports are repaired and emailed automatically.

                Badges

                Special badges show on the player's My stats and badges page and next to their name on the scoreboard. A badge is given by hand (like Tester or Contributor) or earned automatically when a player reaches a milestone you set, like 250 puzzles solved or a 7-day daily streak. Automatic badges need a game account, and are checked whenever the game opens or every few minutes while it is open. The game's own badges (First case closed, Flawless and the rest) are earned on each device and are not listed here.

                Special badges are switched off, so players see none and none are given out. Turn on Special badges under Settings and switches > Play.

                  Supporters

                  The game is free. A Supporter gets a badge next to their name on the scoreboard: Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum. Payment services pick the badge from the membership tier's name or the amount paid (the tier amounts are under Settings and switches > Support and premium); a player shows their highest running badge. Nothing from this page shows in the game until you turn on Show supporter badges and Supporter extras under Settings and switches > Support and premium. Premium also opens the themes ticked under Settings and switches > Support and premium. Payment services add people here by themselves once connected; you can also add someone by hand, for example a GitHub Sponsors or Ko-fi tipper. A grant made out to an email reaches the player the next time they sign in with that confirmed email.
