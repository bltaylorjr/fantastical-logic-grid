Search (Google and Bing)

How people find thelogicgrid.com in Google and Bing over the last 28 days. Fetched once a day (06:41 UTC) and kept on the game's server. Google's numbers arrive two or three days late.

Daily puzzles

Everyone gets the same daily puzzle. Change a coming day's theme, difficulty or size, or ask for a new story. Past days and today never change, and no story is ever used twice. The site is rebuilt every night at 00:17 UTC, so a change shows in the preview and the game after the next build.

To publish a change sooner, run Publish web version on GitHub (Run workflow).

Puzzle quality

Themes and levels that players give up on, rate low, or say are too hard or too easy, worst first. From the anonymous play summaries; a group needs at least 5 games before it is flagged. Fix ideas: add story parts in the Story workshop, switch a theme off for a while, or plan easier daily puzzles.

Story workshop and review

Story workshop

Write story parts that every player's new stories use. The game mixes them with its own, so a story is never repeated. Story parts only change the words, never the logic, and every part is checked by the game's rules first. Added parts reach players within about 15 minutes.

AI story writer

Describe an idea and Google Gemini drafts a few parts to choose from (free tier, using the GEMINI_API_KEY Cloudflare secret). Nothing is added until you press Add.

    Shared by players

    Story parts sent from older versions of the game, and the parts added here. Approved parts reach every player within about 15 minutes. Rejected parts are never shown. Parts are anonymous.
