                          Scoring and race rules

                          Every puzzle you solve earns points, alone or in a race. In a race everyone gets the same puzzle, and the lowest race time wins. You need an account and a screen name to earn points or race. Playing alone never needs one.

                          Once you sign in, the game asks you to choose a screen name (or uses the one your account already has), and it shows at the top of the screen. If you are not signed in, you play as Guest.

                          How points are awarded

                          Points = difficulty base × size × speed × help, rounded, and at least 1.

                          • Difficulty base: Basic 10, Easy 20, Medium 35, Hard 55, Expert 80, Quantum Physicist 110, Theoretical Physicist 165.
                          • Size: bigger grids are worth more. A 3 × 4 grid counts as 1, a 4 × 4 grid as 1.4, a 6 × 7 grid as 3.9.
                          • Speed: matching the usual time for the puzzle (shown on the New puzzle screen) counts as 1. Solving in under about half the usual time earns up to 1.5. Very slow solves still get at least 0.6.
                          • Help: each hint takes away 15% and each check 5%, but you always keep at least a quarter of the points. Each step of a hint (the nudge, the clue, the cell) counts as one hint.
                          • A revealed solution earns nothing, and neither does a puzzle you started over. In a race, Start over clears your marks but the race clock, hints and checks keep counting. Each puzzle counts once, and up to 40 solo puzzles a day count.

                          Race rules

                          1. Start: a race uses your New puzzle choices. Share the 6-character code or link. You choose whether strangers can join through Find a race.
                          2. Join: with the code, the link, or Find a race. A race is open to new players for 7 days and takes up to 20 players.
                          3. Challenges: anyone in a race can challenge a player by screen name. The challenge waits in the game under Play against others until they accept (and join the race) or say no thanks. Each player can be challenged once per race, and you can send up to 30 challenges a day. Players can turn challenges off in their screen name window.
                          4. Race time = solve time + 1 minute per hint + 15 seconds per check. The clock runs while the puzzle is on screen.
                          5. Places go from the lowest race time. Equal times share a place.
                          6. Finishing: your answers are checked, and a result is sent once and cannot be changed. Results count for 14 days after the race starts.
                          7. Giving up: revealing the solution counts as giving up and earns no points. You have 10 seconds to undo it.
                          8. Points: a solved race earns points the same way as a solo puzzle.
                          9. Race wins: you have a win while you are in first place and at least one other player has solved the race. Someone who finishes later with a lower time can take the win.

                          The scoreboard

                          • This week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC. All time never resets.
                          • Players are ranked by points. A tie goes to more race wins, then to whoever scored first.
                          • The top 50 are shown, and your own place is always shown. The board can be up to 30 seconds behind.
                          • Leave the scoreboard (in the screen name window) removes your name, points and race results. To stay signed in you then choose a new name, or you can sign out and play as Guest.
