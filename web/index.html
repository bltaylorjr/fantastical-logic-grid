          AI pictures and voice

          With an OpenAI API key, each new story gets its own painted scene, and characters and the narrator speak in natural voices. Pictures and voices are made once per story and saved on this computer. The key stays on this computer. OpenAI charges your account for each picture and voice clip (roughly a few cents to 20 cents per picture). Without a key, the game uses its built-in art and your computer's own voices.

          AI pictures
          Where voices come from

          Kokoro voices download once from Hugging Face (about 90 MB) the first time the game opens, then stay on this computer. Each character gets a voice that suits their name. Nothing you play is sent anywhere.

          Get a free Google key at aistudio.google.com (Get API key). On Google's free tier, the story and clue text you send may be used to improve Google's products. The free tier has a daily limit; when it runs out, the game uses this computer's voices until the next day.

          Story workshop

          Add your own story parts. The game mixes them with its own to write brand new stories, so a story is never repeated. Story parts only change the words, never the logic, so every puzzle still has exactly one solution.

          Your parts join stories for this topic only, so a story always fits its puzzle.

              How to play The Fantastical Logic Grid

              Captions are on. Use the CC button in the player to turn them off.

              Read the transcript

              Welcome to The Fantastical Logic Grid. Every case is a brand new story, and you are the detective. Each puzzle is a logic grid wrapped in a story. The story sets up a mystery, the clues give you the facts, and the grid is where you work out the answer. Every story is written fresh for you and never repeated. And every puzzle has exactly one answer you can reach without guessing.

              Here's how to play. Start on the New puzzle screen and pick a difficulty. Basic and Easy are perfect for beginners. MENSA is for true masterminds. Next, choose a theme, like Mystery, Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Sports or Pets. Or pick Surprise me.

              Most themes also have topics, like which holiday or which kind of mystery. Pick one, or leave it on Any. Then pick a size. More categories and more items make a bigger, harder grid. Full experience adds scenes, characters and sound. Silent mode keeps only the story, the grid, the clues and the answer key.

              Press Generate puzzle, and your story is written in a moment. Here's our case: The Spooky Key. The buttons along the top help you as you play. Read the story first. It tells you who is involved, and it ends with the question you need to answer. Here, it's: Who arrived at 9:45 PM? You can also press Read the story aloud to hear it.

              Select a character's portrait to hear the clues they remember. Every clue is also in the clue list. On your first Easy puzzle, coach tips appear above the grid to guide your first moves. Now the grid. Each square is where two things meet, like a ghost hunter and an arrival time. Your job is to decide whether they go together.

              Click or tap a square once to mark an X. X means ruled out: these two don't go together. Tap again for an O. O means confirmed: these two are a match. Tap a third time to clear the square. Let's solve it together, starting with the simplest clues. Clue 1 says the ghost hunter who carried the Flashlight arrived at 10:00 PM. Mark an O there.

              Clues 2 and 3 each rule out pairs directly. Mark an X for each one. Clue 4 says the ghost hunter who carried the Flashlight is Felix or Hugo, so no other ghost hunter is possible. Clue 5 says the ghost hunter who carried the Thermometer did not arrive at 9:30 PM. Mark an X there. Clue 6 says Felix arrived at 9:45 PM. Mark an O there.

              Clue 7 says the ghost hunter who carried the Compass did not arrive at 9:45 PM. Mark an X there. Felix arrived at 9:45 PM, so Felix cannot go with any other arrival, and no other ghost hunter can go with 9:45 PM. Each row and each column in a section has exactly one match, so everything else in that line is an X. The ghost hunter who carried the Flashlight arrived at 10:00 PM, so Flashlight cannot go with any other arrival, and no other gear can go with 10:00 PM. 9:15 PM is the only arrival left for Thermometer. Mark an O there. When only one square is left in a row or column, it must be the answer.

              In the full experience, each finished section also reveals more of the scene. Stuck? Press Hint. The first hint points you to a clue. Press Show more for the exact square, and then the full reasoning. You can even let it mark the square for you. Press Check at any time to see whether any of your marks are wrong.

              Made a mistake? Undo and Redo take back any move, as many times as you like. Prefer words to grids? List view lets you choose each match from a menu. The game also works fully by keyboard and with a screen reader. When you think you know, answer the questions under the clues, and press Check answers. Keep going the same way, clue by clue, until the whole grid is filled in. Case closed! Felix arrived at 9:45 PM, so Felix was behind the attic key all along! It was a prank with a bedsheet and a fan. You also see your time and a suggestion for your next puzzle.

              Then rate the puzzle, and say whether it felt too easy, just right or too hard. The game learns from your answers and tunes your next puzzle to suit you. You can print any puzzle, blank or with your marks, with the answer key on its own page. Your progress saves automatically. Come back any time and pick up where you left off, from Saved puzzles. Press Help to see every keyboard shortcut, and to watch this video again.

              Play in light or dark colors, or in high contrast. And Silent mode is always there when you just want the puzzle. You can also play with other detectives. You never need an account to play. But a free account keeps your puzzles, progress and settings in step on the website, the desktop app and your phone.

              Create one with your email and a password. If you ever forget your password, Forgot password sends you a reset link. Once you are signed in, your puzzles sync on their own. Sign in on another device and they are waiting for you. Play against others lets you race other players on the very same puzzle, and climb the scoreboard. First, pick a screen name. It is the only thing other players see, and rude names are turned away.

              To race, press Start a race. It uses the difficulty, theme and size you picked on the New puzzle screen. Share the race code or link with a friend. Or anyone can press Find a race to join an open race. Everyone gets the very same puzzle. The race bar shows the code, and Standings shows who is still solving.

              When you finish, you see your place. Fastest solve wins, and each hint adds time. The scoreboard ranks players this week and of all time. Every solve earns points, even when you play on your own. Finally, make the game your own. Settings shows what the game has learned from your play. You can reset it at any time.

              In the Story workshop you can write your own story openings and endings. The game mixes them into new stories, so every case can include your ideas. That's all you need: read the story, follow the clues, and mark your X's and O's. Welcome to The Fantastical Logic Grid. Happy sleuthing!

              How to play

              8 minutes, narrated, with captions and examples

              Read a clue, then mark the grid. X means two things do not go together. O means they do. Each row in a sub-grid has exactly one O. When the grid is full, answer the story questions.

              Select a character's portrait to hear the clues they remember. Each finished section of the grid reveals more of the scene. Want fewer distractions? Choose Silent mode on the New puzzle screen or in Settings to see only the story, the grid, the clues and the answer key.

              Want company? Under Play against others on the home screen, sign in and choose a screen name, then start a race (everyone gets the same puzzle, fastest solve wins), join one with a code, or find an open race. Your solved puzzles earn points on the Scoreboard.

              O or EnterConfirm
              Delete, BackspaceClear the cell
              RDescribe the row
              Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo, redo

              Ctrl+S, Ctrl+PSave, print

              EscClose a dialog or menu

              Single keys (X, O, H, C, R) can be turned off in Settings. On a Mac, use Cmd instead of Ctrl.
