# Get ready to launch free with optional extras (26 Oct 2026)

The launch package is on your PC in `C:\Users\btay7\Downloads\Projects\The-Fantastical-Logic-Grid`. It holds the website, the Windows installer, the admin app, the promo videos, the docs and a launch checklist. The game is free, and nothing in it is locked or charges money.

Everything below is free to do. Item 2 sets up a way for players to pay you, and every payment service keeps a fee from each payment, so read its pricing page first.

## 1. Pick how the game makes money

**Why:** taking money always has fees, so nothing has been signed up for or connected without your OK.

**Steps:**

1. Open the project thread "Prepare freemium launch package" in Claude and pick an option on the card: a free tip jar with GitHub Sponsors (0% fee), a Ko-fi membership for extras, a Paddle or Lemon Squeezy one-time unlock, or all free for now.
2. The options and fees are written up here: https://github.com/bltaylorjr/fantastical-logic-grid/blob/main/docs/launch/FREEMIUM.md

## 2. If you pick a tip jar: set up the page and switch on the link

**Why:** the game's Menu can show **Support the game**, which opens your page. It's off until you add a link.

**Steps (GitHub Sponsors):**

1. Open https://github.com/sponsors and click **Get sponsored**, then join with your personal account (bltaylorjr).
2. Fill in the profile, add your bank account and tax form when asked, and submit. GitHub reviews new profiles, which can take a few days.
3. When it's approved, copy your page link, which looks like `https://github.com/sponsors/bltaylorjr`.

**Steps (Ko-fi instead):**

1. Sign up at https://ko-fi.com with fantasticallogicgrid@gmail.com, and connect Stripe or PayPal to get paid.
2. Copy your page link, which looks like `https://ko-fi.com/yourname`.

**Then, either way:**

1. Open https://flg-api.btay74.workers.dev/admin/ and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings and switches > Support and premium**.
3. Paste the link in **Support page link** and click **Save**.
4. Turn on **Support the game link**. Within about a minute the game's Menu shows **Support the game**.
