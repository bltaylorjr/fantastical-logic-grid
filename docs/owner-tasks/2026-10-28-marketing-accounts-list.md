# Every site that needs an account for the marketing

This list covers both campaigns: the launch campaign (Launch 2026) and the teacher campaign (Educators 2026). Sites are in order of their first posting day, so do them from the top down. Every one is **free**. Never pay for promotion or press **Boost**: nothing in the plan needs it.

In short: **11 sites post themselves** once their keys are saved (Facebook already does). **9 stay by hand** because the site has no posting API or forbids automatic posts.

Sign up with **fantasticallogicgrid@gmail.com** where you can, and keep every password in a password manager such as Bitwarden (free). Never email passwords or keys to anyone, including me.

"Posts itself" means the autopilot posts your approved ads on their day once the keys are saved as GitHub secrets. Step-by-step key instructions are in admin under **Connections and setup**: <https://flg-api.btay74.workers.dev/admin/#connections>. For LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, TikTok and Reddit, the card there has a **Get the key** button: you paste the app's id and secret, sign in to the site, click Allow, and admin shows the key to save. No command lines. Until a site's keys are saved, its ads come to you by email on the day instead, so nothing is missed.

"By hand" means the site has no posting API, or its rules forbid automatic posts, so you get an email on the day with the words, picture and link to paste.

## Needed this week (first posts 2 to 6 November)

1. **Your own email** (2 Nov, by hand). No new account needed: you send the friends-and-family note from your own email.
2. **Bluesky** (3 Nov, posts itself). Sign up at <https://bsky.app>, handle like **thelogicgrid.bsky.social**. Then make an app password: **Settings** > **Privacy and security** > **App passwords**. Save the secrets `BLUESKY_HANDLE` and `BLUESKY_APP_PASSWORD`.
3. **LinkedIn** (3 Nov, posts itself). Posts go on your own profile at <https://www.linkedin.com>. Make a free LinkedIn Page for the game (LinkedIn needs one to create the app), then follow the LinkedIn card in Connections and setup. Its key lasts 60 days, and the check tells you when to press Get the key again.
4. **Mastodon** (4 Nov, posts itself). Join a general server such as <https://mastodon.social> or a games server such as <https://mastodon.gamedev.place>. Then **Preferences** > **Development** > **New application** (tick `write:statuses` and `write:media`). Save the secrets `MASTODON_URL` and `MASTODON_TOKEN`.
5. **Instagram** (5 Nov, posts itself). Create the account in the Instagram app, switch it to a free **Business** or **Creator** account, and link it to the Facebook Page (Page **Settings** > **Linked accounts**). Then save `INSTAGRAM_ACCOUNT_ID` (steps are in Connections and setup; it reuses the Facebook key).
6. **Threads** (5 Nov, posts itself). Turn it on from the Instagram account in the Threads app. The Threads key needs its own Meta app: steps are in Connections and setup. Save `THREADS_USER_ID` and `THREADS_TOKEN`.
7. **X (Twitter)** (6 Nov, posts itself). Sign up at <https://x.com>. Then open the free developer portal at <https://developer.x.com>, create a project and app with **Read and write**, and save `X_API_KEY`, `X_API_SECRET`, `X_ACCESS_TOKEN` and `X_ACCESS_SECRET`. Only the **Free** plan, never a paid one.
8. **AlternativeTo** (6 Nov, by hand: a one-time listing, no API). Sign up at <https://alternativeto.net> and add the game as an alternative to Puzzle Baron and Brainzilla.

## Needed by mid-November (posts 7 to 18 November)

9. **itch.io** (7 Nov, by hand: no posting API). Sign up at <https://itch.io/register>, then add the game at <https://itch.io/game/new>. Price: free (no donations needed yet).
10. **YouTube** (9 Nov, posts itself after a free approval). Make a channel for the game at <https://www.youtube.com/create_channel>, then follow the YouTube card. Google keeps uploads from a new app private until it passes a free audit, so send the audit form early and save the keys once approved. Captions and playlists stay by hand: the email reminds you.
11. **Facebook groups** (10 Nov, by hand: Facebook stopped letting apps post in groups). No new account: join puzzle, homeschool and teacher groups from your own Facebook and post only where the rules allow, one group a day.
12. **TikTok** (10 Nov, posts itself after a free approval). Sign up at <https://www.tiktok.com/signup>, then follow the TikTok card. TikTok reviews each app (free, about a week) before it may post publicly, so submit it early.
13. **Reddit** (11 Nov, posts itself). Sign up at <https://www.reddit.com/register>, then follow the Reddit card. Join and comment for a week or two first, because many subreddits hide posts from brand-new accounts. Posts that point at a weekly thread (r/homeschool) stay by hand, and replying to comments is always yours.
14. **Pinterest** (17 Nov, posts itself after a free approval). Free business account at <https://www.pinterest.com/business/create>. Make the boards Printable Logic Puzzles, Brain Games, Puzzle Night and Classroom Logic Puzzles, then follow the Pinterest card. Ask for Standard access (free) so pins are public.
15. **Product Hunt** (17 Nov, by hand: its rules forbid automatic launches). Sign up at <https://www.producthunt.com>. New accounts must wait about a week before launching, so do this one early.
16. **Hacker News** (18 Nov, by hand: no posting API). Sign up at <https://news.ycombinator.com/login>. Like Reddit, an older account with a few comments does better.
