# Review the educator marketing campaign (20 draft ads)

A new free campaign tells teachers, schools and districts about the free
printable puzzle packages on thelogicgrid.com/educators/. It has 20 ads,
pictures and a printable flyer with a QR code. Nothing is posted until you
approve it. Every ad has its own tracked link, so the admin Marketing
section shows which ads bring visits, educator requests, confirmed school
emails and packages sent.

## 1. Review and approve the ads

1. Open the admin dashboard: https://flg-api.btay74.workers.dev/admin/
2. Go to **Marketing** and find **Free puzzle packages for educators**.
3. Open each ad. Read the words, the posting day and the notes. Choose
   **Edit** to change anything.
4. For each ad you are happy with, choose **Edit**, set the status to
   **Approved to post**, and save. Leave any you do not want as Draft, or
   delete them.
5. When you are done, choose **Edit** on the campaign and set it to
   **Running**. Approved ads on the automatic channels (Facebook Page, X,
   Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, Instagram) then post on their day. The rest
   are emailed to you on their day to post by hand.

The first ads are dated **Monday, November 9**. Posting days run to
January 5, timed for the weeks before winter break and the January return.

## 2. Accounts and places to post (all free)

You only need these for the ads you approve.

1. **OER Commons** (a free library of teaching resources): sign up at
   https://oercommons.org/ and use **Add OER** on the ad's day.
2. **Pinterest**: add the boards named in the Pinterest ad (Classroom Logic
   Puzzles, Early Finishers, Free Teacher Printables) to the game's account.
3. **Facebook teacher groups**: join a few teacher, homeschool co-op and
   school librarian groups that allow free resources. Post in one group a
   day at most.
4. **Reddit**: read the rules of r/teachingresources and r/homeschool before
   posting.

## 3. Print the flyer

1. Download the flyer: https://github.com/bltaylorjr/fantastical-logic-grid/raw/main/media/marketing/educators-flyer-letter.pdf
2. Print it on US Letter paper and pin it up in staff rooms, school offices,
   libraries, or take it to teacher events. Attach it to the district emails.

Every scan of its QR code is counted for the flyer ad.

## 4. District emails

The "Email to district coordinators" ad is a template for your own email.
Send it one person at a time (no bulk lists), a few a day, to district
curriculum, gifted or library coordinators from their public staff
directory. If anyone asks not to be contacted, note it on the ad and do not
email them again.

## Where to see the results

**Marketing** now has an **Educator requests** table, by ad and by
channel. **Educator requests** shows which channel and ad each request
came from. All of it is anonymous counting with no cookies or personal
data.
