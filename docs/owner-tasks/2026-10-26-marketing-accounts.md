# Set up the free accounts for the launch campaign (about 1 hour, all free)

**Why:** The launch campaign is ready in the admin dashboard under **Marketing**: about two dozen ready-to-post ads (the words, the pictures or video, and a tracked link for each), one per free channel. Posting has to come from accounts you own, so only you can create them. Nothing is posted until you say so. Every one of these is free; none of them needs a card. Skip any channel you don't want: its ad simply stays a draft.

**Tip:** sign up everywhere with **fantasticallogicgrid@gmail.com** and the name **The Fantastical Logic Grid**, so the game's accounts stay apart from your personal ones. Use the logo at https://github.com/bltaylorjr/fantastical-logic-grid/raw/main/web/icons/icon-512.png as the profile picture, and https://thelogicgrid.com as the website.

**First, look at the ads:**

1. Open the admin dashboard, https://flg-api.btay74.workers.dev/admin/, and go to **Marketing**.
2. Open each ad under **Launch on free channels** to read its words. Change anything you like with **Edit**.
3. For each ad you are happy with, choose **Edit**, set **Status** to **Approved to post**, and save. You can also just reply in the project and say which ones are approved.

**Social accounts (5 to 10 minutes each):**

4. **Facebook Page:** while signed in to Facebook, open https://www.facebook.com/pages/create, name it The Fantastical Logic Grid, category **Video game** (or **Games/toys**), and add the website.
5. **Instagram:** open https://www.instagram.com/accounts/emailsignup/ and create the account. In **Settings > Account type and tools**, switch to a free **Creator** or **Business** account. Add https://thelogicgrid.com to the bio.
6. **Threads:** open the Threads app or https://www.threads.net and sign in with the new Instagram account. It creates the Threads profile.
7. **TikTok:** open https://www.tiktok.com/signup and create the account. Switch to a free **Business account** in Settings so the website link can go in the bio.
8. **YouTube channel:** sign in to YouTube with fantasticallogicgrid@gmail.com, open https://www.youtube.com/create_channel and create a channel named The Fantastical Logic Grid. Verify it with a phone number at https://www.youtube.com/verify so longer videos and custom thumbnails are allowed.
9. **X (Twitter):** open https://x.com/i/flow/signup. The free plan is enough for posting.
10. **Bluesky:** open https://bsky.app and choose **Create account**.
11. **Mastodon:** open https://joinmastodon.org/servers and join a general server such as **mastodon.social**.
12. **Pinterest:** open https://www.pinterest.com/business/create/ for a free business account. Create three boards: **Printable Logic Puzzles**, **Brain Games**, **Puzzle Night**.
13. **Reddit:** sign up at https://www.reddit.com/register. Reddit communities want people who take part, not just post links, so spend a few days commenting in r/WebGames, r/logicpuzzles and r/puzzles before the first post.
14. **LinkedIn:** use your own profile. Nothing to create.

**Game sites and directories (5 minutes each):**

15. **itch.io:** sign up at https://itch.io/register. When you're ready, the ad "itch.io game page" has every field to fill in at https://itch.io/game/new. Set the price to **No payments**.
16. **Product Hunt:** sign up at https://www.producthunt.com. New accounts may have to wait about a week before they can launch, so sign up now even though the launch comes later.
17. **Hacker News:** sign up at https://news.ycombinator.com/login (bottom half of the page).
18. **Indie Hackers:** sign up at https://www.indiehackers.com.
19. **AlternativeTo:** sign up at https://alternativeto.net.

**Microsoft Store (optional, 20 minutes):**

20. Individual developer accounts are free. Open https://storedeveloper.microsoft.com, choose **Individual developer**, and sign in with a Microsoft account (btay74@outlook.com works). You'll be asked to verify your identity.
21. Once you have the account, say so in the project and the store package for the web version will be prepared with the free PWABuilder tool for you to upload.

**Not included because they cost money (for later, only if you want them):** Google Play ($25 one-time, and mobile is paused), Apple App Store ($99 a year), and any paid ads or boosted posts.

**When the accounts are ready:** reply in the project with which ones you made and which ads are approved. The posting order and dates are in the campaign plan. After each post, open the ad in **Marketing**, press **Mark as posted** and paste the post's link, and its visits and solves will appear next to it.
