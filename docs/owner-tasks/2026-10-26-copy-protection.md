# Protect the game from copying: check the terms, and optional paid steps

The game now carries a copyright notice, a proprietary LICENSE (it is no longer
"no license"), a Terms of use page, a block on AI-training crawlers and a
legal opt-out from text and data mining. This is general information, not
legal advice. If the game starts earning money, a short talk with an
intellectual-property lawyer is worth it.

## 1. Read the new terms (free, 5 minutes)

Why: they are written in your name, so make sure you are happy with them.

1. Open https://thelogicgrid.com/terms/ (live a few minutes after the merge).
2. Check the owner name ("Bobby Taylor") and what players may do: play, print
   for personal or classroom use, share results, stream or review.
3. Open https://github.com/bltaylorjr/fantastical-logic-grid/blob/main/LICENSE
4. If anything should change (for example, a company name as owner, or no
   classroom printing), reply in the project thread and it will be updated.

## 2. Keep proof that you made it (free)

Why: if someone copies the game, you need to show you had it first.

1. The GitHub history already dates every change. Do not delete the repository.
2. Keep the dated project zips in `C:\Users\btay7\Downloads\Projects`.

## 3. If you find a copy (free)

1. Email the site owner first, pointing to https://thelogicgrid.com/terms/.
2. Copy on GitHub: file a DMCA notice at https://github.com/contact/dmca
3. Copy in Google search results: https://support.google.com/legal/answer/3110420
4. Copy in an app store: Google Play https://support.google.com/legal/troubleshooter/1114905 and Apple https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/appstorenotices/

## 4. Optional, COSTS MONEY: register the copyright (about $45 to $65)

Not needed for protection (copyright is automatic), but in the US you must
register before you can sue, and registering early allows statutory damages.

1. Check the current fee at https://www.copyright.gov/about/fees.html
2. Apply online at https://www.copyright.gov/registration/ (a "Single
   Application" fits one author who owns the whole work).

## 5. Optional, COSTS MONEY: register the trademark (about $350 per class)

Protects the name "The Fantastical Logic Grid" and the logo. Using the name
already gives some common-law rights where you use it, without registering.

1. Search that nobody holds it: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/
2. Check fees at https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/fees-payment-information
3. Apply at https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply (class 9 covers
   downloadable games; class 41 covers online games).

Nothing in sections 4 or 5 will be done for you; they are your call.
