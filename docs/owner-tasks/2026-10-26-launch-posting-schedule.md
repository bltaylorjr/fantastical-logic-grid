# Launch campaign posting schedule: what to post, and when (from Monday, November 2)

**Why:** You approved all 24 launch ads on October 26. They are now marked **Approved to post** in admin > Marketing, and the campaign is **Active**. The ads have to be posted from your own accounts, so only you can post them. Every one is free. The full plan, with every ad's exact words, is in the launch plan PDF that was emailed to btay74@outlook.com, and in your Downloads\Projects folder.

**How to post any ad (about 5 minutes each):**

1. Open the admin dashboard at https://flg-api.btay74.workers.dev/admin/ and go to **Marketing**.
2. Under **Launch on free channels**, open the ad for today.
3. Click **Copy the words**. This copies the post text with its tracked link already filled in. Paste it into the site.
4. Download the picture or video listed under the ad, using the media links in the **Media** part of the page, and attach it to the post.
5. Publish the post. Then copy the post's own web address.
6. Back in admin, click **Mark as posted** and paste the post's address. This lets the dashboard show which posts bring players.
7. If you can, reply to comments within the first hour or two.

**Before November 2:** finish the free account sign-ups from the earlier email "Set up the free accounts for the launch campaign". On Reddit, comment in r/WebGames, r/logicpuzzles and r/puzzles for a few days before posting, so the account isn't brand-new.
